# S14-1C 最小测试台词（压缩版，约 12 分钟 ≈ 2–4 元）

> 外放不戴耳机（顺带测 AEC）。每通电话测完立刻挂断。
> 要记录的数字：页面底部的「静音 ms / 重连 ms / 首音 ms」。

## 通话 1・自由对话（约 8 分钟，6 句话覆盖 G1/G2/G3/G4/G7/G8）



| # | 时机           | 念这句                                                                                              | 测什么                                           |
| - | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| 1 | 接通后          | `Hi! Let's talk about my internship. I ... um ... work on software for a driverless racing car.` | 自动回复、句中停顿、um 不被切                              |
| 2 | 它回答后         | `Yes, I did. Well ... I think ... it was really fun.`                                            | 短回答、句中长停顿不抢话                                  |
| 3 | **它正在说话时打断** | `Wait, tell me your favorite movie.`                                                             | 打断 1：记录**静音 ms**（目标 <500）、**重连 ms**（目标 <1000） |
| 4 | **它再次说话时打断** | `Stop, what were we talking about before I interrupted you?`                                     | 打断 2 + **P0 对账**：它只能说出你实际听到的内容，不能说出没播完的后半句    |
| 5 | **它回答时再打断**  | `Never mind, 我不知道"内卷"英文怎么说`                                                                      | 打断 3 + 中文兜底（应用中文解释 + 给英文）                     |
| 6 | 它解释完后        | `太累了. Yesterday I go to campus and meet my teacher.`                                             | 短中文不被无视 + 语法纠错                                |

→ 点**挂断**：麦克风灯灭、无残留声音 = G7 通过。

## 通话 4・稳定性 G6（功能全过后再做，可先测 15 分钟）

开始时说：`Let's have a long chat. Ask me questions one by one about my hometown, my internship, the AI industry, my hobbies and my study plans.`

它问到时从下面挑对应答案念，没覆盖到的问题随便简答就行，目的是让通话不断：



* **Hometown**：`Guangzhou is a big trade city in the south. It's hot most of the year, and the food is amazing — I often have dim sum with my family on weekends.`

* **Internship / racing team**：`I write perception and control software for a driverless formula car. We use cameras to recognize the track, and my job is to tune the algorithms so the car stays on course at high speed.`

* **AI industry**：`I think AI will change engineering a lot. Already we use it to debug code and read papers faster, but I believe the people who succeed will be those who understand both the technology and the real problem.`

* **Hobbies**：`Besides coding, I enjoy building side projects and watching tech talks. I also like cycling around the city when I need a break from screens.`

* **Study plans**：`I hope to pursue a master's degree in robotics or intelligent vehicles. My long-term goal is to work on systems that combine software with real hardware.`

* **English learning**：`I'm building my own English learning app, actually. My goal is to read foreign papers without a translator and eventually speak fluently in international conferences.`

* **Daily routine**：`I usually get up around seven, attend classes in the morning, and spend the afternoons in the racing team's lab. Evenings are for my own projects and English.`

* **Future career**：`I'd like to join a company working on autonomous driving or robotics. I prefer products where I can see the code move something physical.`

观察：不断线、声音不重叠、内存不持续涨；初验 15 分钟，产品化前补满 30 分钟
