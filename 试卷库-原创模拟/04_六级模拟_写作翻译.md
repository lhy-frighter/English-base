## Section A 写作
::kind writing
### Q1
Directions: For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay on the importance of learning to cooperate with people from different backgrounds. You should write at least 150 words but no more than 200 words.
> model: In an increasingly interconnected world, few abilities matter more than cooperating with people whose backgrounds differ from our own. Such cooperation is valuable precisely because diversity, left unmanaged, easily turns into misunderstanding.
> Working with people from different regions, disciplines or cultures exposes us to assumptions we did not know we held. An engineer may measure a plan by its efficiency; a designer, by how users feel; a partner from another culture may read direct criticism as a personal insult. Learning to bridge these gaps forces us to articulate our reasoning, listen before judging, and translate private certainty into shared language. Teams that survive this process are not merely more pleasant to work in: research on group problem-solving repeatedly shows that they identify more options and catch more errors than homogeneous groups that agree too quickly.
> Admittedly, diverse cooperation is slower and occasionally uncomfortable, yet comfort is a poor guide to growth. The ability to turn difference into collective intelligence is not a social grace but a core professional competence, best learned early through deliberate practice.

## Section B 翻译（中译英）
::kind writing
### Q2
Directions: Translate the following passage from Chinese into English.
中国正在加快建设以国家公园为主体的自然保护地体系。首批国家公园覆盖了从热带雨林到高寒湿地的多种生态系统，保护着大熊猫、藏羚羊等珍稀物种的栖息地。与过去分散的保护区不同，国家公园实行统一管理，力求在生态保护与当地社区发展之间取得平衡。游客只能在划定区域内活动，核心保护区原则上禁止人为干扰。建设国家公园不仅是为了守护生物多样性，也是为子孙后代留下可持续的生态财富。
> model: China is accelerating the development of a nature-reserve system centered on national parks. The first batch of national parks covers a wide range of ecosystems, from tropical rainforests to high-altitude wetlands, and protects the habitats of rare species such as the giant panda and the Tibetan antelope. Unlike the fragmented reserves of the past, national parks are placed under unified management, seeking a balance between ecological conservation and the development of local communities. Visitors may move only within designated zones, while human disturbance is in principle prohibited in core conservation areas. Building national parks aims not only to safeguard biodiversity, but also to leave sustainable ecological wealth for future generations.
