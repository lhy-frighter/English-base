TITLE: Night Buses Return as Cities Rethink the Late Shift
LEVEL: B2
GENRE: 新闻·网站
WORDS: 327

At half past midnight, the number 42 bus leaves the central station with a surprising mix of passengers: hospital cleaners starting their shift, kitchen staff heading home, students, and a nurse eating an apple with the concentration of someone who will not see dinner until dawn. Ten years ago this route stopped at 23:00. This spring, the city brought it back.

The decision reverses a pattern. When night bus networks were cut in the 2010s, the standard argument was arithmetic — low passenger numbers, high operating costs, and ride-hailing apps filling the gap. But transport researchers kept pointing out that the arithmetic hid an inequity. Office workers have metro services until midnight and taxies as an alternative. Night-shift workers — cleaners, security staff, bakers, nurses — often have neither, and they are precisely the people a hospital cannot run without.

The pandemic years complicated the picture by shifting travel to earlier hours, but the recovery of restaurants and events pushed demand back towards late nights. Cities that restored night networks report passenger numbers exceeding old records on weekends, driven partly by a new factor: smartphone ticketing has made occasional use easy for people who would never buy a monthly pass. Money still has to come from somewhere. Several cities now fund night services through a small levy on late-opening venues, on the argument that bars and clubs benefit directly from staff and customers getting home safely. Others accept the deficit and describe it as a public-health measure, citing research on drunk driving and on the pay gap between day and night work.

For passengers, the case is simpler. As one cleaner put it, waiting for the first metro at 05:10 used to add two unpaid hours to her day. The bus takes that away. It will not make headlines like a new metro line, but for the people on board, an ordinary bus at half past midnight is the most useful piece of infrastructure in the city.
